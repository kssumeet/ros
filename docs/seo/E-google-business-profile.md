## 9. Q&A: current status and what to do with the 10 pairs

**Status (checked 28 Oct 2026):**
- Google removed the GBP Q&A feature in **India** (and Indonesia) in Sept 2024. A Google Product Expert gave policy violations as the reason ([Search Engine Roundtable, 4 Sept 2024](https://www.seroundtable.com/chatgpt-ads-manager-plugin-audience-updates-product-feeds-38005.html)).
- Google discontinued the Q&A API on 3 Nov 2025, and in Dec 2025 began replacing Q&A on Maps with an AI "Ask" feature that answers from business information and reviews ([PPC Land, 22 Dec 2025](https://ppc.land/google-quietly-kills-q-a-for-an-ai-button-most-wont-use/)). Forum users report they can't add new questions ([Local Search Forum](https://localsearchforum.com/threads/ask-a-question-button-missing-gone-in-gbps-unable-to-publish-new-faqs.62955/)).
- **Conclusion:** seeding Q&A on the ROS profile is not possible. Don't try workarounds, such as asking friends to post questions.

**Use the 10 pairs as follows:**
1. **Website FAQ.** Put them on `/book-a-demo/` (Q1, Q2, Q9, Q10) and `/contact/` (the rest), plus the relevant service page. Mark them up as `FAQPage` in Rank Math (Yoast: FAQ block). The AI "Ask" feature and Google's AI answers can draw on clear on-site answers.
2. **GBP posts.** Turn 2–3 of them into posts after week 12 (same format as W6 and W9).
3. **Profile fields.** Make sure the description, services and attributes already answer them; the AI "Ask" feature reads those fields.
4. **If Q&A returns in India**, post each question from the owner account and answer it immediately.

- **No incentives:** no discount, gift, free cable, raffle entry or service add-on in exchange for a review, even for an "honest" one.
