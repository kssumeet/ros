**Will acoustic panels soundproof my room?**  
No. Acoustic panels absorb sound inside the room, which reduces echo and makes dialogue clearer. Soundproofing stops sound travelling to other rooms, and that needs mass, airtight seals and decoupled walls, floors or ceilings. Many rooms need some of each; the assessment tells you which. *(45 words)*

**Can you soundproof a room completely?**  
Complete silence is rarely realistic in an existing building. How much isolation is possible depends on the walls, slab, doors, windows, ventilation and how loud you play. **Do you set up recording and podcast studios?**
