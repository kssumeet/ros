# Extract of the Arobuz SEO brief (sections the helpers implement)

## E. Google Business Profile pack
- **Categories.** Primary category: Home theater store. Secondary: Audio visual consultant, Home automation company, Soundproofing service [plus any other real Google categories that fit; check they exist].
- **Business description:** up to 750 characters, no URLs, no prices, opening with what ROS does and where.
- **Services list:** every service, each with a description of up to 300 characters.
- **Products section:** 8 product-category entries with short descriptions.
- **Attributes and date:** the attributes to switch on, and the opening date (2011).
- **Photo plan:** 30+ photos by type (exterior with signage, demo room, team, installs, products), with file names and captions.
- **Posts:** 12 Google posts for the first 3 months, one a week (offers, demo slots, project updates, guides). Each is under 1,500 characters and has a button type.
- **Q&A:** 10 pairs to seed, written from real buyer questions.
- **Review programme:**
  - a WhatsApp review-request message, in English and Hinglish versions
  - an email version
  - short card/QR text for the demo room
  - 5 reply templates: 5-star, 4-star, mixed, negative, no-text
  - Follow Google's rules: ask every customer, no incentives, no gating.

- **Tracker table** with columns Platform, URL to check, What to change, Priority, Status. Cover: Justdial, Sulekha, IndiaMART, Yappe, Bing Places, Apple Business Connect, Facebook page, Houzz, Google Maps, Magicpin, TradeIndia, Asklaila, Ranchi business directories, HiFiVision dealer listings, and the brand dealer locators (Q Acoustics, ViewSonic, Artsound).
- **Paste-ready text:** the exact NAP block, plus a short and a long business description for directories.
- **Duplicates:** the steps to merge or remove duplicate listings that use the Kanke Road address or rosdc.in.
- **Local links:** 15 realistic local link opportunities (Jharkhand business associations, Ranchi real-estate developers and interior designers, architect directories, local news and lifestyle sites, brand partner pages), with the pitch angle for each.

## H. Content plan and briefs
- **3-month calendar:**
  - **Month 1:** "What does a home theatre cost in India?"; "Room size for Dolby Atmos: a practical guide".
  - **Month 2:** Projector vs TV for a home theatre; Soundbar vs home theatre; How to soundproof a room in India; 5 home theatres we built in Ranchi.
  - **Month 3:** Living-room home theatre setups; Choosing recliners for a home cinema; Boardroom AV checklist; Acoustic panels: what they fix and what they don't.
- **A brief for each of the 10 pieces:** target keyword and secondaries, search intent, title and meta, H2 outline, questions to answer, internal links, a table or visual to include, word count, and the CTA.
- **Phase 3 city pages:** a template for Jamshedpur, Dhanbad and Bokaro that avoids doorway-page duplication (unique local projects, travel and site-visit details, local FAQs). These go live only once ROS has real work in those cities.

## Rules
- Do not invent facts, client names, reviews, ratings, prices, project counts or search volumes. Use a visible [placeholder] for anything unknown.
- Client names: use them only where marked "[with permission]".
- Use Indian English and ₹/lakh.
- Follow Google's guidelines on local businesses and reviews, and flag anything that could risk a Business Profile suspension.
